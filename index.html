I'm a Frontend Developer specialising in Angular, with 2+ years of professional experience building enterprise-grade applications across retail POS, e-commerce, and digital ordering.

At Retail Technology Company I'm the primary frontend developer on Prism V2.5, a web-based point-of-sale platform built for large-scale retail operations, where I own 19 feature modules end to end — from Touch POS and Transaction V2 through inventory, invoicing, and accounting. The work I'm proudest of is the tender and checkout engine: I integrated 7+ payment gateways (Tamara, Tabby, Mada, MyFatoorah, STC Pay, Al Rajhi, and bank cards) alongside split payments, item- and ticket-level discounts, and coupon handling, which reduced average checkout time by 40%.

I care as much about the architecture underneath the features as the features themselves. I led our migration to Angular 21 with zoneless change detection and standalone components, and designed a reusable NgRx SignalStore layer with custom store features — pagination, request status, query caching — now adopted across the product in place of duplicated state logic. I also built the HTTP interceptor chain handling authentication, response caching, offline detection, and Sentry tracing, which helps sustain 99.9% uptime in production.

Beyond the POS platform I've shipped four further production products — Meraki QR Menu, Samah Abaya, BMask, and the Retail-Tec corporate site. Each ships full Arabic / English internationalisation with RTL support, something I've come to treat as a first-class requirement rather than an afterthought.

  • Angular 21 — standalone, signals, zoneless, NgZone, SSR
  • NgRx SignalStore, RxJS, interceptor chains & caching
  • Payment gateway integration & Arabic / English RTL
