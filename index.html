I build the software people stand in front of — point-of-sale terminals, checkout flows, and the storefronts behind them.

Most of it is Angular. Most of it ships to retail businesses in Egypt and the Gulf. And most of it comes down to one thing: moving money from a customer to a merchant without anything going wrong — on a till with a flaky network, in two languages, through seven different payment providers that each have their own opinion about how a transaction should work.

I started as a trainee in 2023 and now lead the frontend of a POS platform used to run large-scale retail operations. I care about the parts nobody demos: the interceptor chain, the caching layer, what the screen does when the connection drops mid-sale.
